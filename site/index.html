What powers the game

This is an educational strategy model. It is not measured El Pitirre 3 performance, not a race prediction and not a rules compliance check.

Energy model

Three 8-hour track days: day 1 from 10:00 to 18:00, days 2 and 3 from 09:00 to 17:00, with charging from 07:00 to 20:00. Sun position is for Gravois Mills, Missouri in mid-July. Panel power is area × irradiance × cell efficiency × 0.93. Driving demand is air drag, rolling resistance, corner braking, a hill-loss term and 25 W of electronics, divided by drivetrain efficiency. Battery charge and discharge are each 97% efficient. With a flat battery the car slows to what the sun can sustain; below 10 mph it parks and charges to 15%. Only whole laps before each day's flag count.

Pitirre 3: documented and assumed

Documented in SERRT's FSGP 2027 Mechanical Preliminary Vehicle Design Report (11 November 2026): four wheels with a three-phase motor on the left-rear wheel; 198 in (5.03 m) long, 83.29 in (2.12 m) wide, 55.32 in (1.40 m) to the top of the canopy; 128 in (3.25 m) wheelbase; cockpit 113 in from the nose; 10.68 in tyre radius; 53.9% of static weight on the front axle. The report uses a deliberately overestimated weight of 1,000 lb including the driver for its load cases, and the game uses that figure as the car's mass, so the real car should be lighter. From the team's sponsor presentation: 288 Samsung 50S 21700 cells in 32s9p, 115.2 V nominal, 45 Ah, which gives 5.184 kWh nominal, not measured usable energy.

Assumed, because neither document gives them: drag area (0.14 m² at the real dimensions), panel area (set to the 6 m² reference limit), rolling resistance, cell efficiency and drivetrain efficiency. When you reshape the body the game scales drag with width, height and length and adds weight by rules of thumb: 180 Wh/kg for the pack, 3 kg/m² of panel. Panels fit on 78% of length × width, and a driver needs at least 0.8 m of height.

Weather, rivals and track

Race-day cloud wanders around the forecast and may bring one cloud bank or sun break a day. Decision cards project outcomes from the forecast, so the real sky can still surprise you. Rivals carry the names of schools on the 2026 FSGP entry list. Their cars, speeds and tactics are invented, and no real result or endorsement is implied. The organizer names Ozarks International Raceway and a 3.97-mile course for FSGP 2027. The circuit drawn here is illustrative, and on-screen laps are sped up.

Rules

The 2026 Rev B and 2025 Rev C profiles carry array, battery and size limits from those documents. 2026 Rev C supersedes Rev B. Free design removes the caps. Reliability, traffic, penalties, tire changes and battery temperature are not modelled.

UPR Mayagüez · Pitirre 3

Built by students. Driven by purpose.

SERRT, the Solar Engineering Research Racing Team, brings engineering, finance and marketing students together to build solar race cars and promote renewable energy across Puerto Rico. The story runs from Shining Star in 1989 to El Pitirre 2, sixth at FSGP 2019 with the Spirit of the Event and Fastest Egress awards, and now Pitirre 3, in design for FSGP 2027.
